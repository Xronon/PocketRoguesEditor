# Changelog

## 1.8 — first public release

- Gold, skill points and attributes of every hero; levels of all 54 skills.
- Gear of a hero with a run in progress (fortress, dungeon floor, Camp and other special
  locations): quality, effect tiers, adding and removing effects by the game's rules, rings by
  their own point rules, removing curses, the game's own effect descriptions.
- Adding, replacing and deleting items; new gear and rings come legendary.
- A `.reg` backup before every write, undo for every edit, a check that the game has not changed
  its data since the window was opened.
- English and Russian, following the game's language or chosen by hand.
